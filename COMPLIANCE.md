# Compliance Matrix

This document maps superCPE behavior to the 2026 Statement on Standards for
CPE Programs (`docs/2026-Statement-on-Standards-for-CPE-Programs.pdf`) and
nothing else. Each row ties a Standards locator to the feature that satisfies
it, the code that implements it, and any known gap. Rows are added as features
ship; an empty table means no Standards-touching feature has shipped yet.

| Locator | Requirement | Feature | Where in code | Gap |
|---------|-------------|---------|---------------|-----|
| 3.01.1 | "Learning activities provided by CPE program sponsors must specify the knowledge level, content, and learning objectives … Knowledge levels consist of Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Update, and Overview." | 002 | `backend/app/constants/knowledge_levels.py`; validation rule 9 in `backend/app/services/packages.py`; CHECK constraint in `backend/app/models/lesson_package.py` | Ingest enforces the five levels on stored lessons. The participant-facing surface that communicates level, content, and objectives now exists (004's public course page) but is unreachable until a course is published (008). |
| 3.02.1 | "All programs identified as Intermediate, Advanced or Update must clearly identify prerequisite education, experience, and advance preparation in precise language … For courses with a program knowledge level of Basic and Overview, prerequisite education or experience and advance preparation, should be noted if applicable, otherwise, state 'none' in the course announcement or descriptive materials." | 002 | Validation rule 10 in `backend/app/services/packages.py`; `LEVELS_REQUIRING_PREREQUISITES` and `PREREQUISITES_NONE` in `backend/app/constants/knowledge_levels.py` | superCPE refuses blank prerequisites/advance preparation for Intermediate, Advanced, Update and stores the literal "None" for blank Basic/Overview. It cannot judge whether stated prerequisites are "precise language"; that is the content reviewer's call (feature 008). The course announcement that displays them is 004's public course page, unreachable until publish (008). |
| 3.01 | "Sponsored learning activities must be based on relevant learning objectives and outcomes assigned to the learning activities that clearly articulate the professional competence that should be achieved by participants." | 004 | `course_objectives` in `backend/app/services/courses.py` derives a course's objectives from its lesson packages, grouped by lesson in position order; they are shown per lesson on the admin course page and in the public 8.01 payload (`backend/app/routers/courses.py`) | Objectives are authored in video-tool and cannot be edited in superCPE; a course with wrong objectives is fixed by re-exporting the lesson. Not participant-reachable until publish (008). |
| 3.02.1 | "CPE program sponsors must develop and execute learning activities in a manner consistent with the learning objectives, prerequisite education, experience, and advance preparation of participants" — stated per course, precisely. | 004 | Attach and update-version refuse a package whose `field_of_study`, `knowledge_level`, `prerequisites`, or `advance_preparation` disagrees with the course's, naming the field and both values (`_check_agreement` in `backend/app/services/courses.py`); the agreed value is stored on `courses` and re-copied on every attach | The admin cannot type course-level values at all, so the catalog can never contradict the lessons. Page unreachable until publish (008). |
| 7.01.1 | "For learning activities in which segments are classified in multiple fields of study, the CPE credits granted should first be computed based on the content time of the total program. Next, the CPE credits granted should be allocated to the fields of study based on the field of study content time." | 004 | Attach refuses a package whose `field_of_study` differs from the course's (`backend/app/services/courses.py`) | Deliberate gap: superCPE refuses multi-field courses rather than allocating credit across fields. A course has exactly one field of study until there is a reason to build the allocation. |
| 8.01, 8.01.1 | "CPE program sponsors must provide descriptive materials that enable participants to assess the appropriateness of learning activities … must make the following information available in advance: 1) Learning objectives 2) Type of formal learning program 3) Recommended CPE credit and recommended field of study 4) Prerequisites 5) Program knowledge level 6) Advance preparation 7) Program description …" | 004, 005 | Public `GET /api/v1/courses` and `GET /api/v1/courses/{course_code}` (`backend/app/routers/courses.py`, published courses only) and the `/courses/:code` page (`frontend/src/pages/CoursePage/`) carry title, description, objectives by lesson, level, prerequisites, advance preparation, field of study, and lessons with durations; 005 added item 3's `recommended_credit` with its `credit_basis` line (`public_credit` in `backend/app/services/credit.py`), served as null — and the page row omitted — while the stored credit is stale or below the minimum awardable | Not yet reachable: nothing is published until 008, so the route correctly serves nothing. Item 2 (type of program) and items 8–11 (registration, refund, complaint resolution, sponsor statement) are feature 011's policies pages. |
| 8.01.2 | "All programs must clearly identify prerequisite education, experience, and advance preparation requirements, if any, in the descriptive materials. Prerequisites, if any, must be written in precise language so that potential participants can readily ascertain whether they qualify for the program." | 004 | The public course payload and page always carry `prerequisites` and `advance_preparation` (never omitted; "None" is stated per 3.02.1), sourced from the packages the course's lessons agree on | Whether the language is "precise" is the content reviewer's judgment at sign-off (008). Unreachable until publish. |
| 7.02.7 | "If the entire self study program constitutes a video, then the prescribed word count formula … would consist of the actual video time plus the total number of review questions … multiplied by 1.85, divided by 50" — the duration term must be "the actual audio/video duration time (not narration of the text)". | 002 | `backend/app/services/ffprobe.py`; validation rules 4–5 in `backend/app/services/packages.py`; `duration_source = 'measured'` CHECK in `backend/app/models/lesson_package.py`; boot check in `backend/app/main.py` | The duration is measured by ffprobe on the server against the file that was uploaded, and a manifest claiming anything but `measured` is refused. superCPE cannot verify video-tool's claim that the narration inside the file was rendered from measured audio; it relies on that attestation and refuses packages that do not make it. Credit computation from this duration is a later feature. |
| 9.02.1(8), 9.02.2(7) | "Program materials." — among the required documentation elements CPE program sponsors must retain as evidence of compliance (9.01: for a minimum of five years). | 002 | `lesson_packages` stores the manifest, questions, and transcript verbatim (`backend/app/models/lesson_package.py`); the video is retained at `video_key` via `backend/app/storage.py` | Retention is currently a local disk under `backend/uploads/`; five-year durability (DigitalOcean Spaces, backups) is not implemented yet. Nothing is ever deleted, but nothing enforces the retention period either. |
| 9.01 items 1, 8, 9, 10, 11 | Certificates of completion must include "1) CPE program sponsor name … 8) NASBA sponsor identification number 9) State registration number, if required by the state boards 10) NASBA time statement stating that CPE credits have been granted on a 50-minute hour 11) Any other statements required by boards of accountancy". | 003 | `sponsor_profile` and `sponsor_state_registrations` in `backend/app/models/sponsor.py`; `TIME_STATEMENT` and `CERTIFICATE_SPONSOR_FIELDS` in `backend/app/constants/certificate.py`; `missing_fields()` gates issuance; admin editing via `backend/app/routers/admin_sponsor.py` | superCPE is not on the National Registry, so item 8 cannot be satisfied today: `registry_status` is `not_registered`, and the rule is that while it is, `national_registry_id` must be empty (CHECK constraint plus validation) and no response, page, or document may include the words "National Registry" or a sponsor ID. This feature enforces that on its own responses only (`SponsorProfilePublic` drops the field); every later feature that renders sponsor facts must read `may_claim_registry`. No certificate is rendered yet (feature 010). |
| 9.01.1 | "The entity whose name appears on the certificate of completion is responsible for awarding the CPE credits earned by a participant." | 003 | `legal_name` on `sponsor_profile` (`backend/app/models/sponsor.py`) records the responsible entity; it may equal `name` | Nothing renders a certificate yet, so no name "appears" anywhere; feature 010 must print the entity this row records. |
| 9.02 | "CPE program sponsors must retain adequate documentation (electronic or paper) for a minimum of five years to support their compliance with these Standards and the reports that may be required of participants." | 003 | Nothing is ever deleted (packages from 002, the sponsor profile here), which satisfies the floor by default | The five-year period is not yet recorded anywhere in code; feature 011 adds the constant alongside the audit bundle. |
| 7.01 | "Sponsored learning activities are measured by actual program length, with one 50-minute period equal to one full CPE credit … rules and regulations of state boards of accountancy may differ on acceptable increments … 3)(i) Traditional … Self Study: A minimum of one-fifth or one-half credit may be awarded initially … credits may be awarded in one-fifth increments". Sponsors "may round down (but not up)". | 005 | `MINUTES_PER_CREDIT`, `CREDIT_INCREMENT`, `MIN_AWARDABLE` in `backend/app/constants/credit.py`; `round_down` in `backend/app/services/credit.py` floors to one-fifth uniformly and returns 0.0 below the minimum awardable | One-fifth is applied uniformly; the per-jurisdiction increment policy state boards may require is roadmap 019 (comment at `round_down`). |
| 7.02 | "For self study programs, CPE credit must be based on one of the following educationally sound and defensible methods: Method 1: Pilot test … Method 2: Computation using the prescribed word count formula". | 005 | Method 2 is the implementation: `compute` in `backend/app/services/credit.py`; nothing accepts a typed or pilot-tested credit | Deliberate gap: Method 1 (7.02.1–7.02.4) is not implemented at all — superCPE does not pilot test and refuses to hold a half version of it. A pre-program assessment does not exist in the package contract, so its exclusion from the computation is vacuous today. |
| 7.02.5 | "The prescribed word count formula begins with a word count of the number of words contained in the text of the required reading … and should exclude any material not critical to the achievement of the stated learning objectives … Review questions, exercises, qualified assessment questions … should not be included in the word count." | 005 | Only the manifest's `word_count` enters the word term (`compute` in `backend/app/services/credit.py`); the stored transcript is never counted, and questions are counted separately in the question term, never as words | Gap: `word_count` is trusted from the video-tool manifest. superCPE cannot verify that number against the package text, nor that video-tool excluded non-critical material; the attestation is recorded in the stored manifest. |
| 7.02.6 | "[(# of words/180) + actual audio/video duration time + (# of questions × 1.85)] /50 = CPE credit … The total number of review questions (including those above the minimum requirements), exercises, and qualified assessment questions … the CPE credits granted must be rounded down." | 005 | `compute`/`store` in `backend/app/services/credit.py` with the NASBA numbers in `backend/app/constants/credit.py`; questions of both kinds are counted from each package's stored `questions` JSON; the award and its per-lesson inputs are stored on `courses`; every course mutation through `touch` recomputes, and derived staleness (`is_stale`) refuses stale use | Adaptive-path averaging (7.02.6 second paragraph) is out of scope: superCPE has no adaptive programs. Terms are truncated at two decimal places of a minute so the retained record re-adds exactly and can never round a credit up. |
| 7.02.7 | "If audio and video segments … constitute additional learning … the actual audio/video duration time may be added … If the entire self study program constitutes a video … there would be no word count for text used in the formula." | 005 | Per lesson, `av_is_additional_learning` selects the branch in `compute` (`backend/app/services/credit.py`): true counts the ffprobe-measured `duration_seconds` in the A/V term; false counts `word_count` in the word term and the duration not at all. The all-video case falls out as a zero word term | The flag is video-tool's attestation from the manifest (002 rule); superCPE cannot judge whether the audio is in fact additional learning rather than narration. |
| 9.02.2(2)(ii) | For method 2, "the word count formula calculation, as well as the supporting documentation for the data used in the word count formula (for example, word count; number of review questions, exercises, and qualified assessment questions; duration of audit or video segments … and actual calculation), must be retained." | 005 | `credit_breakdown` on `courses` stores one row per lesson with every input (duration, flag, words, questions by kind); `from_stored` and `as_text` in `backend/app/services/credit.py` rebuild the written-out calculation from the stored columns alone, without the packages | The record is retained in the database but not yet exported: the audit bundle that hands it to a reviewer is feature 011. |
