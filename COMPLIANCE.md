# Compliance Matrix

This document maps superCPE behavior to the 2026 Statement on Standards for
CPE Programs (`docs/2026-Statement-on-Standards-for-CPE-Programs.pdf`) and
nothing else. Each row ties a Standards locator to the feature that satisfies
it, the code that implements it, and any known gap. Rows are added as features
ship; an empty table means no Standards-touching feature has shipped yet.

| 7.01 | "Sponsored learning activities are measured by actual program length, with one 50-minute period equal to one full CPE credit … rules and regulations of state boards of accountancy may differ on acceptable increments … 3)(i) Traditional … Self Study: A minimum of one-fifth or one-half credit may be awarded initially … credits may be awarded in one-fifth increments". | 7.02 | "For self study programs, CPE credit must be based on one of the following educationally sound and defensible methods: Method 1: Pilot test … Method 2: Computation using the prescribed word count formula". | 005 | Method 2 is the implementation: `compute` in `backend/app/services/credit.py`; nothing accepts a typed or pilot-tested credit | Deliberate gap: Method 1 (7.02.1–7.02.4) is not implemented at all — superCPE does not pilot test and refuses to hold a half version of it. | 7.02.5 | "The prescribed word count formula begins with a word count of the number of words contained in the text of the required reading … and should exclude any material not critical to the achievement of the stated learning objectives … Review questions, exercises, qualified assessment questions … should not be included in the word count." | 005 | Only the manifest's `word_count` enters the word term (`compute` in `backend/app/services/credit.py`); the stored transcript is never counted, and questions are counted separately in the question term, never as words | Gap: `word_count` is trusted from the video-tool manifest. superCPE cannot verify that number against the package text, nor that video-tool excluded non-critical material; the attestation is recorded in the stored manifest. |
| 7.02.6 | "[(# of words/180) + actual audio/video duration time + (# of questions × 1.85)] /50 = CPE credit … The total number of review questions (including those above the minimum requirements), exercises, and qualified assessment questions … the CPE credits granted must be rounded down." | 005 | `compute`/`store` in `backend/app/services/credit.py` with the NASBA numbers in `backend/app/constants/credit.py`; questions of both kinds are counted from each package's stored `questions` JSON; the award and its per-lesson inputs are stored on `courses`; every course mutation through `touch` recomputes, and derived staleness (`is_stale`) refuses stale use | Adaptive-path averaging (7.02.6 second paragraph) is out of scope: superCPE has no adaptive programs. | 7.02.7 | "If audio and video segments … constitute additional learning … the actual audio/video duration time may be added … If the entire self study program constitutes a video … there would be no word count for text used in the formula." | 005 | Per lesson, `av_is_additional_learning` selects the branch in `compute` (`backend/app/services/credit.py`): true counts the ffprobe-measured `duration_seconds` in the A/V term; false counts `word_count` in the word term and the duration not at all.
